# Hose logo concept

Created 2026-10-25 with the built-in image generation tool.

Hose makes renting a GPU and running an open AI model approachable: pick a model, turn it on, chat or generate pictures, and let it turn itself off when idle. The proposed mark uses a hose-like lowercase **h** to suggest flow and easy control. Forest green comes from the app's existing `--accent` token in `src/style.css`.

This is an initial raster logo concept. App icon packaging and UI integration are pending review.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a standalone logo symbol for Hose, a native macOS app that lets ordinary people run their own open AI chat and image models on rented GPUs, with easy on/off control and automatic idle shutdown.
Primary request: Create a distinctive, beautifully simple lowercase h monogram made from a flowing hose-like tubular stroke. The h must read instantly. A tall straight left stem rises above one generous rounded arch; the right leg ends at the baseline. Give the silhouette an understated sense of flow and an approachable, confident personality. The metaphor is AI on tap, ownership and ease.
Style/medium: precision flat graphic brand mark, vector-friendly geometric silhouette, restrained contemporary Mac utility identity. Solid single-color forest green #1f5c4a. Rounded outer corners and subtly rounded stroke terminals. Consistent substantial stroke weight and broad open negative space. Optically balanced, strong at 24 pixels.
Composition: one mark only, centered, square canvas, approximately 66% canvas height, generous transparent margins. Actual alpha transparency.
Constraints: no wordmark or text, no background tile, no gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, sparkle, robot, brain, cloud, circuitry, water droplet, literal faucet, decorative rings, thin lines or mockup. Original simple silhouette. Professional final logo artwork.
